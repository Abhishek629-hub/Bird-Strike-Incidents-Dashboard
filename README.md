# Bird-Strike-Incidents-Dashboard

This dashboard provides insights into bird strikes involving airplanes in the USA, leveraging historical data to enhance aviation safety. It visualizes key trends, such as:
Frequency & Locations: Identify hotspots where bird strikes occur most often.
Aircraft & Bird Species Impact: Analyze which aircraft types and bird species are most involved.
Time & Seasonal Trends: Discover patterns based on time of day, season, and altitude.
Damage Assessment: Evaluate the severity of incidents and associated costs.
